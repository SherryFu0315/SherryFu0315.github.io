Two axes. Across: what kind of AI the study is about. Down: whether it intervenes — building something that makes people better at the work — or observes what AI set off once it arrived. Click an axis to fall into it; click a star to go to the study.

Where I file each study. The constellations are real ones — Ursa Major, Cassiopeia, Orion, Lyra, Corvus, Crux — and the grey points are the places still open in each cell.

Where the literature files them. Every faint star is a work one of these studies cites; the bright ones are the studies. No position here is chosen by hand — a force simulation runs over the real citation graph, so two studies sit close together only when they draw on the same references, and a work several papers lean on is pulled into the space between them. 608 works cited, 25 of them by two or more studies.
