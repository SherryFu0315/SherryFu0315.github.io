# Image prompts

One image per project. Save each as `<id>.jpg` and hand over the folder;
the id is what the site already uses, so nothing needs renaming.

**Append to every prompt:**

> editorial photography, natural light, clean uncluttered composition, subject centred with
> generous margin, shallow depth of field, cool muted palette, 3:2 landscape —
> no text, no logos, no watermarks, no brand marks, no identifiable faces

**Output:** 3:2 landscape, at least 1600px on the long side, JPG or PNG.
